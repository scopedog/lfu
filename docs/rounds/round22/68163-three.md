## `de2957d5` — a field on neither page

`llapi_scan_device(3)` says the parameter structure "is documented in
`llapi_scan_namespace(3)`", and that page walks it field by field —
`sp_size`, `sp_max_depth`, `sp_thread_count`, `sp_flags`, `sp_padding`,
`sp_stats`, `sp_want`, `sp_filter` — with `sp_search` missing. So the page a
reader is *sent to* for the structure did not describe all of it.

`sp_stats` was already the model for this: unused by a namespace scan and
given a sentence there anyway. `sp_search` now has the matching one.

## `a93f237a` — a message written to a reviewer

Four paragraphs justified the patch against an earlier revision of itself:
"routing every failure here made `lfind --device /dev/sdb99` answer …",
`SCAN_CHUNKS_MAX` "without a literal here to update", "-EBUSY names both of
its sources **rather than asserting** the pool is imported". On master there
is no earlier revision and no `scan_backend_kind()` routing anything, so a
reader from `git log` has nothing to compare against.

Rewritten to state the rule once and its consequences: **a leading slash
separates a path from a dataset name.** A name with one is stat'ed and
answers `-ENOENT`/`-ENOTDIR`/`-ELOOP`/`-EACCES` before a backend is chosen,
even without ZFS; a name without one is a dataset and never reaches the
stat; so `scan_backend_kind()` needs no slash case, and `-ENOTSUP` keeps the
meaning the man page gives it and `conf-sanity 165` keys its skip on. The
`SCAN_CHUNKS_MAX` and `-EBUSY` paragraphs now say what they are rather than
what they are not.
