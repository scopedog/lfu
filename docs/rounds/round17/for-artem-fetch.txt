Notes:
- These refs are the patchsets live as of 2026-09-01, and every one of the
  nineteen changes holds jenkins Verified+1 on them.  They are not stable:
  the next round moves them, and a stale ref fetches an old patchset
  silently rather than failing.  Check the tip hash above against what you
  get if it matters.
- The two branches share no history, so they cannot be checked out into one
  tree as-is.  Combining them conflicts in lustre/tests/sanity.sh, where
  160aa-160ad and 160y/160z sit adjacent and share a closing tail --
  stripping the conflict markers leaves a file that does not parse.
- Any change number works the same way, e.g. refs/changes/56/68156/15 for
  the ldiskfs scanner and everything under it.

What changed in round 17, if you have the round-16 tree already:
- llapi_scan_device() gained no new API, but struct llapi_scan_rec gained
  sr_owner_fid and LLAPI_SCAN_OWNER at the END of the structure, so a
  consumer compiled against round 16 keeps its field offsets.
- lfs find --changelog now counts a nameless record as undecided rather
  than matching it against "", and refuses -uid/-gid/--mdt-count/--mdt-hash
  under --resolve where the record cannot answer them.
- A foreign layout smaller than 32 bytes is no longer refused, so --foreign
  sees files it previously missed.
- lfind --device --fid2path against a mount of another filesystem is now
  refused rather than answered with that filesystem's paths.
