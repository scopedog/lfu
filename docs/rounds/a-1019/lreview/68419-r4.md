### 2. `Documentation/man1/lfs-find.1` (line 874)

(minor) The cookie can anchor a --changelog search too: llapi_find_since() applies lfsc_startrec from fp_since_cookie whatever the source, and 160Ac runs `--changelog $FSNAME-MDT0001 --resolve --since-cookie`. This paragraph only names --since as the way to say where to start, though, and the --since-cookie entry above begins "Like --since", which reads as if it applies only to the verified search. If the patch is refreshed, maybe "--since or --since-cookie then says where in it to start", plus a sentence in the --since-cookie entry saying it works with --changelog as well.
