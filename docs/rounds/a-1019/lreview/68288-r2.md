### 2. `lustre/utils/liblustreapi_pfind.c` (line 4918)

(suggestion) The reason given here only holds for an MDT, but the refusal also applies to an OST target, where the mount is used for nothing except llapi_fid2path_at(). That lookup already handles a fileset: ll_fid2path() sends the mount root's FID as gf_root_fid, and mdt_path_current() stops there, so the answer is relative to the subdirectory mount and "%s/%s" with fds_mnt_path gives a correct local path. An owner outside the fileset comes back as -ENOENT and is counted as nameless.

As written, a node that only has a subdirectory or nodemap-fileset mount can't use --fid2path on a stopped OST at all, even though it would work. The target kind isn't known here yet. One option is to remember the fileset result and refuse only when the pre-pass actually ran (fds_dirmap_built), i.e. on an MDT.
