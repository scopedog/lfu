=== FAIRNESS NOTES ===
- Their series is patch set 1, self-described on the ticket as AI-generated and
  untested by the author, and currently Verified: REJECT in Gerrit.  These are
  measurements of a design at an early stage, not a verdict on finished code.
- They report fewer objects (regular files only), which is *less* work per
  scan; their rate is if anything flattered by the narrower scope.
- Their pull transport yields the device between batches by design; some of the
  gap is that deliberate politeness, not inefficiency.
- Our rate is an in-kernel discard loop; step 4 measured our ring +
  copy_to_user + userspace core at ~0 marginal cost (796k vs 793k), so it is a
  fair proxy for our end-to-end.  Their llapi number already includes their
  full transport to userspace.
