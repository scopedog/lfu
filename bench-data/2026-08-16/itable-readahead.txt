A clean U.  Below ~32 blocks readahead is a large PESSIMISATION: sb_breadahead()
submits REQ_RAHEAD immediately before the blocking sb_bread() of the current
block, so the critical read queues behind its own prefetches.  Above the
latency x rate product it pays.

Readahead and threads buy the same thing -- queue depth -- so they do not add,
and past 4 threads the bookkeeping makes it marginally worse.

=== VERDICT ===
Readahead confirms the diagnosis (the path is latency-bound; more outstanding
I/O helps) but cannot fix it: it overlaps the per-inode round trip instead of
removing it.  +19% at one thread, nothing at four, -5% at eight, and a wrong
depth costs 3x.  Not worth a tuning knob.  The remaining 2x needs the other
half of §8: parse whole itable blocks instead of one iget per inode, which is
what Option 1 does to reach 1.4 GB/s with a single thread.
